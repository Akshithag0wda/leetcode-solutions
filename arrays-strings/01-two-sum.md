# Two Sum

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

## Approach

I used two nested loops to check every possible pair of numbers in the array. If the sum of two numbers equals the target, their indices are returned.

## Complexity

**Time Complexity:** O(n²)

**Space Complexity:** O(1)
