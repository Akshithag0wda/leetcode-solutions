# Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock

**Difficulty:** Easy–Medium

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/best-time-to-buy-and-sell-stock/

## Approach

I kept track of the minimum price seen so far and calculated the profit for each later price. The maximum profit found during the scan is returned.

## Complexity

**Time Complexity:** O(n)

**Space Complexity:** O(1)

## Notes

The solution was tested locally with two test cases and was accepted on LeetCode.
