# Longest Common Prefix

**Difficulty:** Easy–Medium

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/longest-common-prefix/

## Approach

I compared the characters of all strings from left to right. The comparison stops when the characters are different, and the matching characters form the longest common prefix.

## Complexity

**Time Complexity:** O(n × m)

**Space Complexity:** O(m)
