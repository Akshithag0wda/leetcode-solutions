# Reverse a Linked List

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/reverse-linked-list/

## Approach

I used three pointers: previous, current, and next. The links are reversed one by one until the current pointer reaches the end of the list.

## Complexity

**Time Complexity:** O(n)

**Space Complexity:** O(1)

## Notes

This was completed as the bonus linked-list problem. The solution was tested locally with two test cases and was accepted on LeetCode.
